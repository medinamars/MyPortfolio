Privacy Policy

AnimeTTL — Video Content Published on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X and Other Web Platforms

Last updated: July 09, 2026

1. Introduction

AnimeTTL ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you view, like, comment on, or otherwise interact with our video content on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X (formerly Twitter), and other web platforms (collectively, the "Platforms").

2. Information We Collect

We do not operate our own video hosting or account systems. Because our content is published through third-party Platforms, we generally do not collect personal information directly. However, the following information may be collected or accessible:

  • Platform Data: The Platforms on which our content appears may collect information such as your username, profile picture, and viewing activity in accordance with their own privacy policies
  • Comments and Interactions: Content you post in comments or messages on our videos, which we may read and moderate
  • Analytics: Aggregate, de-identified statistics provided by the Platforms (e.g., views, likes, watch time, audience demographics) that help us understand how our content performs
  • Contact Information: Information you voluntarily provide if you contact us directly (e.g., by email), such as your name and email address

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create, publish, and improve our video content
  • Understand audience preferences and engagement
  • Respond to your inquiries, comments, and messages
  • Comply with legal obligations and Platform requirements

4. Cookies and Tracking

We do not set our own cookies or tracking technologies. The Platforms on which our content appears may use cookies, analytics, and advertising technologies in accordance with their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of the Platforms you use to understand how your data is collected and processed there.

5. Third-Party Platforms

Our content is published through third-party Platforms, each of which has its own privacy practices and policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these Platforms. When you interact with our content, the relevant Platform's privacy policy also applies to you. We recommend reviewing:

  • YouTube's Privacy Policy
  • Instagram's Privacy Policy
  • TikTok's Privacy Policy
  • X (Twitter)'s Privacy Policy

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share or disclose information only in the following circumstances:

  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, regulation, or legal process
  • Protection: To protect the rights, property, or safety of ourselves, our viewers, or others
  • With Consent: When you have given us explicit consent to do so

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable law. Aggregate analytics provided by Platforms are retained in accordance with the Platforms' own retention policies.

8. Children's Privacy

Our content is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or delete it, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Your continued interaction with our content after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
